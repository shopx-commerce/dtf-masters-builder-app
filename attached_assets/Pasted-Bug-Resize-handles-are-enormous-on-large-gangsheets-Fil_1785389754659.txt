Bug: Resize handles are enormous on large gangsheets
File: client/src/components/preview-section.tsx

Root Cause
Handle size is computed as 5 * (dpiScaleRef.current / zoom) canvas-buffer pixels, which always comes out to ~5 screen pixels regardless of artboard size. This is fine for a 12" sheet where previewDims.width ≈ 750px and a 3" design is ~92px wide on screen. But for a 150" tall sheet, previewDims.width shrinks to ~90px (to preserve the tall aspect ratio), so a 3" design is only ~11px wide on screen — and a 5px handle covers 45% of it.
