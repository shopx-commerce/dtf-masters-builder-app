Do not change the orientation logic inside the packers themselves. Judging orientation by where a design's ink ends rather than where it starts was tried and reverted: it fixed one case but cost film on others (21.3" to 22.8" on a test sheet), because a greedy pass cannot know which design will end up setting the sheet's length. That is why the fix is a post-pass that only accepts a change when the film actually gets shorter.
