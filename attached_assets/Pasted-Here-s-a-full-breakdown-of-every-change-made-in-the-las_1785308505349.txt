1. Multi-Select Sizing (W/H Inputs)

2. Multi-Select Drag-Resize Wall Clamping
File: client/src/components/image-editor.tsx

Rewrote handleMultiResizeDelta entirely
Old behavior: computed unclamped positions then group-shifted after the fact — designs could still escape the artboard boundary
New behavior: analytically computes the maximum allowed scaleRatio across all four edges for every selected design, applies that single clamped ratio uniformly — all designs stop together at the wall
3. Alignment + Rotation Panel (Desktop Toolbar)
File: client/src/components/image-editor.tsx

Added a new panel to the desktop toolbar Row 2, after the Duplicate group
Contains two alignment buttons: Vert (center vertically) and Horiz (center horizontally), using AlignCenterVertical and AlignCenterHorizontal icons from lucide-react
For multi-select, alignment snaps to the group centroid (not always canvas center)
Contains a rotation display showing the current rotation degree
Four quick-preset buttons: 0° / 90° / 180° / 270°
New callback handleSetRotation(deg) applies absolute rotation to single or all selected designs, with position clamping
Panel only renders on desktop when at least one design is selected
